The Reframe

Tiger Woods doesn't carry his own bag

Last time I documented this, I'd figured out I was sitting on a distribution channel — 500-1,200 people/month flowing through SD nightlife, and I control where they go.

Then my mentors pushed one level deeper:

Tiger spends every hour being the world's best golfer. That's what drives the sponsorship money. Everything else gets offloaded so the rare thing — his focus — stays protected.

Billy IS the product. The relationships, the judgment, the real-time problem-solving when something goes sideways at midnight — that's what nobody else can replicate.

So the build question shifted: not how do I scale me, but what do I offload so I can be more me?

The Experiment

One page. One form. One signal.

I set my own deadline: landing page live by Feb 14. Not because mentors assigned it — because I said I would.

THE HYPOTHESIS

SD tourists searching for nightlife help will find the page, fill out the form, and convert — without cold outreach, without ads.

Success looks like: form submissions, not just traffic. Tiers, not just names.
